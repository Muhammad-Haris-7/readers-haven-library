Digital Services

Everything we offer beyond the bookshelves.

Online Catalogue

Available: 24/7

Steps:

  1. Open the library catalogue page.
  2. Search by title, author or subject.
  3. Reserve the book and collect it within 3 days.

E-books

Available: 24/7 to members

Steps:

  1. Log in with your membership number.
  2. Choose a title and tap Borrow.
  3. Read online or download for 14 days.

Study-Room Booking

Timings: 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM

Slots: 2-hour slots

Steps:

  1. Ask at the front desk or book online.
  2. Pick a free room and time slot.
  3. Show your membership card on arrival.

Printing and Scanning

Timings: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Charges: PKR 10 per printed page, scanning free

Steps:

  1. Bring your file on a USB drive or email it to us.
  2. Tell the desk how many pages you need.
  3. Pay at the counter.

Free Wi-Fi

Timings: During all opening hours

Network: ReadersHaven-Free

Steps:

  1. Connect to the network "ReadersHaven-Free".
  2. Open a browser and accept the terms.
